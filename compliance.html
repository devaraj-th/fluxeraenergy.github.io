Where BESS Compliance Goes Wrong

The most common compliance failure in BESS projects isn't the testing itself — it's the sequence and scope. Developers commission testing too late, use the wrong laboratory, test the wrong configuration, or discover that their vendor's existing certification doesn't cover the specific cell lot being shipped.

A UL 9540 certificate on a product datasheet does not mean your project is UL 9540 compliant. Certification scope is configuration-specific. A 2022 IEC 62619 certificate may not cover the updated BMS firmware version in your 2026 delivery. These gaps are routine — and routinely missed.

Fluxera's compliance infrastructure maps your project's specific requirements, coordinates testing at the right labs in the right sequence, and manages documentation so that nothing is discovered at the permitting stage.

Testing Coordination Infrastructure

Physical testing isn't just a vendor responsibility. Fluxera coordinates the testing programme across cell, module, and system levels — ensuring the right tests are conducted, at the right labs, in the right order.

Cell & Module Level (IEC 62619 / UL 1973)

Abuse testing protocol selection — nail penetration, overcharge, external short circuit, thermal stability. We select the lab, manage the test schedule, review the raw data, and validate the test report against project specifications.

System Level (UL 9540 / IEC 62933)

Full BESS system certification readiness — electrical safety, fire protection integration, installation compliance, and grid interface requirements. We map exactly which test modules apply to your configuration.

Fire Safety Testing (NFPA 855 / UL 9540A)

Large-scale fire testing coordination for projects requiring AHJ approval or insurance underwriting compliance. Including suppression system validation and separation distance verification.

Laboratory Selection & Management

Not all accredited labs have equivalent BESS experience or capacity. We maintain working knowledge of lab backlogs, specialisation, and quality track record — and match your project to the right facility.

Documentation & Certification Dossier

Complete test report compilation, certificate registry, configuration-specific compliance mapping, and lender/AHJ-ready documentation packages — structured for both Indian and international stakeholders.

Parallel Testing Optimisation

We identify which test protocols can run simultaneously without impacting results validity — reducing total certification timeline without compromising scope coverage.

Standards We Work Within

We operate across the primary international and Indian safety, performance, and compliance standards for stationary battery energy storage systems.

IEC 62619

Safety — Secondary Lithium Cells & Batteries

Cell and battery-level safety testing baseline. Cell-specific — your certification scope must match the exact cell lot and chemistry being deployed. We verify this match before any contract is signed.

UL 9540

Energy Storage Systems & Equipment

System-level safety certification. Required by fire marshals and building departments globally. Covers electrical safety, thermal event containment, and installation compliance.

UL 1973

Batteries for Stationary Applications

Module and pack-level performance and safety. Validates the battery unit between cell and full system — a layer often skipped in compressed procurement timelines.

NFPA 855

Stationary Energy Storage Systems

The reference standard for fire safety, separation distance, and suppression requirements. Our physical infrastructure design team works directly against NFPA 855 requirements.

IEC 62933

Electrical Energy Storage Systems

Performance evaluation methodology for grid-connected storage. Required for bankability assessments, off-taker performance reporting, and lender technical reviews.

Indian Standards

BIS / AIS Applicable Standards

Including AIS-156 for battery pack safety, IS 16046 for lithium cells, and evolving BIS requirements for grid-connected BESS in India — particularly relevant for government and PSU projects.
