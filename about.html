We Saw the Same Infrastructure Failures Repeating. We Built a Company to Stop Them.

Fluxera Energy was founded by engineers who had spent years working across the BESS supply chain — in battery testing, compliance coordination, and project infrastructure — and had watched the same preventable problems destroy project timelines and budgets on repeat. This company exists because the infrastructure layer for battery energy storage systems was being built wrong, and no one was specialising in doing it right.

The Founding Problem

Every major BESS project deployment we examined had the same structural gap. The battery systems were improving. The vendors were credible. The procurement processes were becoming more sophisticated. But the layer between the battery vendor's scope and the project owner's expectations — the physical infrastructure, the compliance programme, the independent technical intelligence — was routinely treated as someone else's problem.

It usually belonged to whoever was cheapest. A general contractor's mechanical subcontractor for the piping. A vendor-supplied certification dossier reviewed by a procurement manager who couldn't evaluate its scope. A fire safety consultant who knew the standard theoretically but had never tested suppression against a lithium thermal event.

We founded Fluxera to own that gap — as specialists with deep BESS-specific knowledge across three disciplines that had to work together: intelligence, compliance, and physical infrastructure.

What we believe

"Infrastructure failures in energy storage are not accidents. They are the predictable result of treating specialist work as a commodity, and of optimising the one layer — vendor selection, contract price — while under-specifying everything that holds the project together."

The founding principle behind everything Fluxera does.

Why Tumakuru. Why 2026. Why Now.

Tumakuru isn't an arbitrary location. Karnataka's energy storage ambition is real — the state has committed to significant renewable integration, BESS procurement is accelerating through KREDL and KERC, and the industrial base in the region means proximity to the early adopters that matter.

We chose to launch in 2026 because the market is at exactly the right moment: sophisticated enough that our clients understand what we're solving, early enough that we can set the infrastructure standard before the market scales past the point where poor-quality infrastructure becomes embedded practice.

India's energy transition will require hundreds of gigawatt-hours of storage. Most of it will be built in the next decade. Getting the infrastructure layer right in the first cohort of large-scale projects sets the reference standard for everything that follows. That's the window we're working in.

Long-term vision

BESS Today. Hydrogen Infrastructure Tomorrow.

The skills and disciplines that underpin Fluxera's BESS capabilities — electrochemical safety expertise, compliance infrastructure management, specialist physical systems — are directly transferable to the emerging green hydrogen infrastructure sector.

We are building our hydrogen infrastructure capability in parallel with our BESS work. Our first hydrogen intelligence reports will be available to Founding Partners in Q3 2026 — covering electrolyser vendor assessment, hydrogen storage safety standards, and the compliance infrastructure for green hydrogen projects in India.

This isn't a pivot. It's the natural extension of the same founding thesis — the infrastructure layer for energy transition technologies needs specialists, not generalists.
