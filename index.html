Built on Standards, Not Assumptions

We don't quote standards to look credible — we work within them every day. Here's what each one means for your project.

IEC 62619

Safety for Secondary Lithium Cells & Batteries

The international baseline for cell-level and battery-level safety requirements. Required for most utility and commercial BESS projects globally. Without this, your equipment cannot be legally deployed in regulated markets.

UL 9540

Standard for Energy Storage Systems

System-level safety certification covering electrical, fire, and installation requirements. Required by AHJs in the US and increasingly referenced in Indian project specifications. Fluxera coordinates the full certification path.

UL 1973

Batteries for Stationary Applications

Covers battery module and pack-level performance and safety for stationary storage. Validates that individual modules meet the safety threshold before system integration — a step many projects skip to save time.

NFPA 855

Standard for Stationary Energy Storage Systems

The fire protection and installation standard for BESS in the US, and the global reference for fire safety engineering. Defines separation distances, suppression requirements, and fire rating criteria — all directly applicable to physical infrastructure design.

IEC 62933

Electrical Energy Storage Systems

Covers performance evaluation methodology for grid-connected storage. Critical for post-deployment performance audits, bankability assessments, and long-term compliance reporting to off-takers and lenders.
